# Cottage of the Breeze

A stair is waiting in the corner of your kitchen, and you can't help going down it. Why?

That is the question this little game asks. It has a quiet town, a tavern that is cozy but wary of you, a shopkeeper who is glad you keep going, eight cellar floors, and at the bottom a Key Warden that asks you for the rule it keeps, and would rather give you what it gives than fight you for it. It is one file. Open it in a browser and you're playing: no install, no account, no key.

## How to move

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys, or W A S D | Walk one tile at a time. Walk into a monster to strike it. |
| E, F, Space or Enter | Do the one thing the big button says: talk, open a chest, trade, go through a door or stair, or fight. They also turn the pages of a note, close someone's speech, close the shop and close the end card. |
| Q | Whisper: a rumour from the world. |
| B | Open your bag, where you wear gear and use potions. |
| O | Explore on your own (autoexplore). |
| V | Turn the fog of war on or off. |

On a phone, a floating joystick walks you and one big button does whatever it says, with your Bag beside it; the controls can be made bigger or switched for left hands. Whisper and autoexplore are keyboard-only for now. The Menu holds your journal, books, album, settings, and Dusk and Dawn.

## A first walk

You start in the cottage with ten gold. The stair down is right there, in the corner of your own kitchen, but the town is worth a walk first: step onto the doorway in the bottom wall and press F to go outside. Say hello to everyone you pass; people stand where they live and work, so walk around them. The Trader buys what you find and sells a few useful things, so visit before you go down.

Down there it's dark and you only see a little way. Every monster you beat gives experience, and enough of it makes you stronger. On the eighth floor the Key Warden is waiting. The rule it keeps is scratched into the stone by the stair: read it, keep it, and it gives you what it gives. Behind it is a little vault; read what is in it, and the way home is right there. The town will have changed a little when you get back.

Things you can collect: gear for five slots (hand, body, head, feet, charm), potions, books and notes, and an album of stickers that fills as you meet things. Nothing is ever lost or missable for good.

## What's still rough

We'd rather tell you than have you wonder. The town is new pixel art; the cottage, the tavern, the cellar and its creatures are still the older paintings, shrunk down, until their pixel versions are drawn. The hero steps one tile at a time. The sounds are a few soft synthesized notes (switch them off in Menu > Display). A few album stickers can't be found yet, and there is no quick way home from deep floors except the vault's stair. Acts 2 and 3 are planned and are not in this release: reading the note in the vault ends this part of the story, and you can keep exploring deeper. Every name and line of story you read is a draft that Rylee has not signed off on. If you connect a small model (the VEFR guide shows how), people make up fresh lines. Your bag, gear, album and journal are saved in this browser.

## Who made it

Rylee and Claude made the game, the words and the pictures. The pictures were drawn with AI image tools (Codex, and MiniMax for some drafts) from our own descriptions; every picture keeps a note of how it was made. A few tiles come from the VEFR project, shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. The fonts are Cinzel and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. The game runs on VEFR, a game for making games.

If you get as far as you can, tell Rylee what you think the stair means. We're still deciding, and we'd like to hear yours.
