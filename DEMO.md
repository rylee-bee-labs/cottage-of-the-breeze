# Cottage of the Breeze

A stair is waiting right outside the cottage door, and you can't help going down it. Why?

That's the question this little game asks. It has a quiet town, a tavern that is cozy but wary of you, a shopkeeper who is glad you keep going, and six dark floors below. It is one file. Open it in a browser and you're playing: no install, no account, no key.

## How to move

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys, or W A S D | Walk one tile at a time. Walk into a monster to strike it. |
| E, F, Space or Enter | Do the one thing the big button says: talk, open a chest, trade, go through a door or fight. They also turn the pages of a note, close someone's speech and close the shop. |
| Q | Whisper: a rumour from the world. |
| B | Open your bag. |
| O | Explore on your own (autoexplore). |
| V | Turn the fog of war on or off. |

On a touch screen, the buttons on the screen do the same things: one big button says what it will do, and four small ones are Talk, Whisper, Bag and Explore. The Menu button holds your journal, your books, the settings, and Dusk and Dawn.

## A first walk

You start in the cottage. Step down onto the doorway in the bottom wall and press F to go outside. Say hello to everyone you pass with E. The Trader buys what you find and sells what you need, so visit before you go down. The stair is in the town's top left corner.

Down there it's dark, and you only see a little way. Rats, damp shades, nests of moths and rustles are guarding things worth having, and the deeper you go the tougher they get. Every monster you beat is worth some experience: enough of it and you reach the next level, and you hit harder and last longer. A cloudy potion heals you, so open your bag with B when you need it. Come back up, sell what you found, and read the notes you picked up. They add up to something.

## What's still rough

We'd rather tell you than have you wonder. The hero steps one tile at a time, with a small hop, and has no walking frames yet. The three deepest floors are new and a bit plain, and nobody guards their stairs yet. There is no sound. There are no equipment slots, and the cat by the rug doesn't notice you yet. There is no ending yet either: we're writing it now.

People talk in their own written lines. If you connect a small model (the VEFR guide shows how), they make up fresh ones. Your bag and journal are saved in your browser, so they stay with this browser.

## Who made it

Rylee and Claude made the game, the writing and the pictures. The pictures were drawn with an AI image tool (Codex) from our own descriptions and references, as proofs, and they are not cleared for training other models. The doors, stairs and a few floors are tiles from the VEFR project, shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. The fonts are Cinzel and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. The game runs on VEFR, a game for making games.

If you get as far as you can, tell Rylee what you think the stair means. We're still deciding, and we'd like to hear yours.
