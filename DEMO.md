# Cottage of the Breeze

A stair is waiting right outside the cottage door, and you can't help going down it. Why?

That is the question this little game asks. It has a quiet town, a tavern that is cozy but wary of you, a shopkeeper who is glad you keep going, six cellar floors with guardians and keys, and a room at the bottom that opens when the Cellar King falls. It is one file. Open it in a browser and you're playing: no install, no account, no key.

## How to move

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys, or W A S D | Walk one tile at a time. Walk into a monster to strike it. |
| E, F, Space or Enter | Do the one thing the big button says: talk, open a chest, trade, go through a door or stair, or fight. They also turn the pages of a note, close someone's speech, close the shop and close the end card. |
| Q | Whisper: a rumour from the world. |
| B | Open your bag, where you wear gear and use potions. |
| O | Explore on your own (autoexplore). |
| V | Turn the fog of war on or off. |

On a touch screen, the buttons on the screen do the same things. The Menu button holds your journal, books, album, settings, and Dusk and Dawn.

## A first walk

You start in the cottage. Step onto the doorway in the bottom wall and press F to go outside. Say hello to everyone you pass. The Trader buys what you find and sells a few useful things (a shovel, an apron, a helm, boots), so visit before you go down. The stair is in the town's top left corner.

Down there it's dark and you only see a little way. Every monster you beat gives experience, and enough of it makes you stronger. Some floors end in a stair that is locked: a Keybearer (a great pale moth, a mushroom elder, an armoured beetle) keeps the key somewhere on the floor, asleep until you bump into it. The Cellar King, a very large crowned rat, keeps a crown seal. Open the sealed door on his floor with it, read what is inside, and the way home is right there.

Things you can collect: gear for five slots (hand, body, head, feet, charm), potions, books and notes, and an album of stickers that fills as you meet things. Nothing is ever lost or missable for good.

## What's still rough

We'd rather tell you than have you wonder. The hero steps one tile at a time. The sounds are a few soft synthesized notes (switch them off in Menu > Display). Act 2 and 3 are not here yet: reading the note under the King's seal ends this part of the story, and you can keep exploring. Every name and line of story you read is a draft that Rylee has not signed off on, and the guardians have plain descriptions instead of names. If you connect a small model (the VEFR guide shows how), people make up fresh lines. Your bag, gear, album and journal are saved in this browser.

## Who made it

Rylee and Claude made the game, the words and the pictures. The pictures were drawn with AI image tools (Codex, and MiniMax for some drafts) from our own descriptions; every picture keeps a note of how it was made. A few tiles come from the VEFR project, shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. The fonts are Cinzel and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. The game runs on VEFR, a game for making games.

If you get as far as you can, tell Rylee what you think the stair means. We're still deciding, and we'd like to hear yours.
