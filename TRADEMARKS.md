(MPL-2.0) and the creative content (CC BY-SA 4.0). They do **not** grant the name **Cottage of the Breeze**, its logo, or
its character names as identity for your own project.

If you fork or remix it, give your version its own name and say it is based on Cottage of the Breeze. The official
source is this repository under `github.com/rylee-bee-labs`.
