# Guardians brief: the Cellar King's court

`status: draft` — Rylee picks; nothing here is approved by a machine. Distribution: we may ship what we generate, with attribution (`AGENTS.md` art rule).

## Purpose

The cellar has five or six floors. Each floor ends with a guardian who carries the key to the stair down; the last is the Cellar King (already drawn, `source/round8/monsters-and-boss/cellar_king_v1`). They are the King's court, weakest to strongest, and their ranks are the names Rylee picked on 2026-10-02: the Reeve, the Bailiff, the Warden, the Marshal, the Seneschal.

## Look

Same world as the King and the round 7 and 8 monsters: painted storybook, soft darker-brown outlines (never black), muted earth tones with the dusk teal `#3F7E84` as an accent, front-facing three-quarter view, friendly-spooky and never gory, big shapes and few details. Each is bigger than an ordinary monster (they are drawn at 256 x 256 px, like the King) and carries ONE visible sign of its rank: a ring of keys, a bell, a lantern, a helmet, a candle and a ledger. Each must read at small size by its silhouette. Transparent or plain teal background, no text.

5. **The Seneschal** (floor 5, the King's right hand): a tall, thin shade in a steward's long robe, holding a single candle in one hand and a heavy ledger in the other, hollow glowing eyes, calm and formal.
