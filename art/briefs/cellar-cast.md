# Cellar cast brief: more kinds of ordinary monster

`status: draft` — Rylee picks; nothing is approved by a machine. We may ship what we generate, with attribution (`AGENTS.md` art rule).

## Purpose

The cellar has four ordinary monster pictures (rat, shade, moths, rustle) plus the mushroom sprite (round 8, unused). Random floors will pick monsters from per-depth tables, so each depth wants its own cast. Eight new creatures, two batches of four: shallow pests, then deeper things.

## Look and size

Same world as the King's court and the round 7 and 8 monsters: painted storybook, soft darker-brown outlines (never black), muted earth tones with dusk teal `3F7E84` accents, front-facing three-quarter view, friendly-spooky and never gory, big simple shapes, few details. Ordinary-monster size (cut to 128 px later), so each must read by its silhouette and ONE feature. Plain teal background, no text. Reference images, in order: the mushroom creature (scale and line weight) and the Cellar King (the world's style).

## Batch `cast_shallow` (floors 1-3), COUNT 4, four different creatures
1. **Cellar spider**: a plump, fuzzy, many-legged spider with a dusty cobweb draped like a scarf and two big curious eyes.
2. **Crock mimic**: a round clay storage jar with a wide grin, two eyes peeking over the rim and little stubby clay feet.
3. **Wick sprite**: a small living candle stub, a flame for hair, melted wax drips for arms, a mischievous smile.
4. **Cellar toad**: a squat warty toad wearing a tiny upturned flowerpot as a helmet, calm and heavy-lidded.

## Batch `cast_deep` (floors 4-6), COUNT 4, four different creatures
5. **Cave cricket**: a big pale cricket with very long curled antennae and a hunched, ready-to-spring pose.
6. **Barrel mimic**: a small wooden barrel with iron hoops, slat-plank teeth in a crooked mouth, two glowing eyes in the bung-hole, short wooden legs.
7. **Root tangle**: a knot of pale glowing roots shaped loosely like a crouching creature, one bright eye in the middle.
8. **Cobweb wraith**: a ragged hooded figure made of grey cobweb, empty hood with two soft glowing eyes, trailing threads instead of feet.

The pictures in a batch come back in any order; identify each by looking and rename.
