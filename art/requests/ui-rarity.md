## The style contract (paste this into every call)

> True Game Boy Advance era pixel art in a warm storybook palette (like The Minish Cap and Mother 3). Crisp hard-edged square pixels, every pixel visible, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients. 1-pixel dark warm-brown outline (never pure black). Limited warm palette: umber, rust, amber, cream, soft moss green, with dusk teal #3F7E84 as the accent. Light from the top left. No ground shadows. Classic top-down RPG view, never isometric. Creatures are cute storybook mobs, not naturalistic animals. No text, letters, numbers, labels or frames anywhere. Nothing from Castle of the Winds.
