A small chest sits open under a ring of old red wax, and a note lies folded on top of the gems.

* * *

"You made it down. Good. Sit a moment; the stairs are long.

The old tavern kept one room for travellers. The town was afraid of what a kind room might cost, so it put a fire over the room, and a seal over the fire, and called that safe.

Nothing down here was ever as unkind as the sealing. Take what you found. Take it up. Kindness is lighter than it looks, and it knows the way home."

* * *

There is no name. The wax is still a little warm.
