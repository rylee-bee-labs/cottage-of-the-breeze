A water-stained tab, kept behind the shop counter. The ink has run,
but the top line still reads like a name.

* * *

Most of the entries are drink. The last one is not: *two rooms, no
charge, keep the fire in* - and then nothing.

* * *

The old sign over the door is gone. Someone in town will still tell
you it read *The Breeze*, if you ask the right way.
