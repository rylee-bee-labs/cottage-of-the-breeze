Scratched into the warm stone beside the stair, in a hand that was not
careful with letters:

* * *

You do not take what is kept.

* * *

Underneath, in the same hand: *It will ask you for this. Keep it, and it
gives you what it gives.*

* * *

Nobody has added to it.
