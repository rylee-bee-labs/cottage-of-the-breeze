Under the lid of the open chest, folded small, there is a note.

* * *

Whoever you are: you are further down than I got.

I kept the rule the warden keeps. You do not take what is kept. That is all of it.

It will ask you for the rule. Not in words, or not only in words. Stand still, and say what you came down for.

When it has given you what it gives, do not stay. Go up.

When you are at the top again, the town will look the same, and it will not be the same. Let them see you go down again.

* * *

It is not signed. The ink has not run.
