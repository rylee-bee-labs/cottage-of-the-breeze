---
title: Writing a Book
kind: book
---
Every book in a world is a plain text file in the pack's `library/`
folder. You write every word; the engine only keeps and shows them.

* * *

The top of the file says how the book is found:

- **shelf**: here from the start, like this one.
- **map**: lying on a tile you name with `at: [x, y]`.
- **resident**: handed over by a `speaker`.
- **earned**: waiting for a moment, like the `bell`.

* * *

A line with three stars on it, like the one above this page, starts a
new page. That's the whole trick. Replace this book with your own.
