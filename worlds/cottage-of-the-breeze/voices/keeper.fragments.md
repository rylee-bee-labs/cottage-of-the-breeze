The Spell Keeper's offline whisper bank. The packaged composer
re-splices these when a game is woven with no pool and no model:
bullet lines speak, everything else is a note to the author.
Keep them short, plain, in this voice. Two lines minimum, or
the composer keeps the honest silence instead.

- Back already? The kettle's still warm.
- I kept a cake back. Cinnamon and honey, like always.
- You'll be down there a while. I'll mind the shop.
- The town talks. I don't listen to all of it.
- Come up slow. There's no hurry down here.
- Some things you find, you don't get to un-know.
