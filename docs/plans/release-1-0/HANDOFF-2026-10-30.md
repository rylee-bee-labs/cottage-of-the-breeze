## What we learned

- **Balance has to be measured across a floor, not a fight.** One retune made each fight fair, yet floors 1-4 cost about 5.5 health bars. Wear per four-floor segment, checked against the bot, is the measure that worked. A fall resets the floors, so floors 5-8 must fit in one life from the landing.
- **The bot is only evidence when it plays like a player.**
  - A bot that walked on while monsters bit fell 49 times on numbers a player rarely falls on.
  - The negative control had been passing for the wrong reason since the engine moved the way down.
  - Both are fixed; keep the negative control honest when the engine moves.
- **Test the whole path from pack to fight, not each half.** The descent's twins agreed on every monster's `guard` and `gold`. The enemy entry between plan and fight then dropped both. The cellar never paid coins until vefr#591.
- **Check the game's real layout.** The quick bar looked right in the fixture pack. In this game's follow-camera phone layout it slid under the message line. Screenshots of the real build caught it.
- **Rolled loot is heavy in a save.** A floor with a dozen rolled drops pushed its explored map out of the 1.5 KB floor budget, so it is 3 KB now.
