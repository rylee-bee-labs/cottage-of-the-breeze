# Acts 2 and 3: a plan (agent draft, 2026-10-04; Rylee decides)

Rylee asked (2026-10-03) for three acts with unique monsters and effects, and chose to ship a very high quality Act 1 first. **Acts 2 and 3 were left for later by her decision; they remain part of the game.** This is the plan for them, not a build, and nothing here says Release 1 fell short. Fire is the first effect (chosen 2026-10-04). Story, names and themes are hers: everything about content below is a proposal.

## What is true today
- Act 1 is the cellar: town, tavern, cottage, six floors, the King's room. Ending and Keybearer ladder are built (release 1).
- The engine plays one act. **Act advance is unbuilt** (VEFR ADR 0006); `complete-act` records an ending but starts nothing.
- Equipment, locks, rule saves, Blueprint, growth and the album (slice 1, if landed) exist.
- **Status effects do not exist.** `vefr/design/elemental-and-status-effects.md` is the approved design (types, statuses with a duration and a tick, resistance); it says an ADR amendment comes before code, because enemy records are closed key sets.

## What each act needs
| Piece | Act 2 | Act 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Zone (her call) | a new set of floors reached from the King's room or town after the ending | a third zone, deeper or different in kind |
| Floors | generated by `vefr delve`, dressed with new tiles (Codex) | same |
| Keybearer ladder | 3 to 4 floors, a new boss in place of the King | same, a final boss |
| Unique monsters | 8 to 10 new families (Codex), none reused from act 1 | 8 to 10 more |
| Effects | 3 statuses (suggested: burning, chilled, stunned) carried by named monsters and a few items | 2 more plus resistances on bosses |
| Gear | 6 new items per act (Codex), slots as today | same |
| Album | stickers per act | same |

## Engine work, in order (each tests-first, foreman-built, review by hand)
1. **Act advance** (ADR 0006): `complete-act` with a target act hands the hero to the next act's start region; saves keep the bag, level and gear. Needed by both acts. Cottage release-1 uses it with no change to its rules.
2. **Status effects, slice 1**: `statuses` block (id, duration, tick), enemy `inflicts` on hit, hero ailments shown in the HUD in text and shape (never colour alone), cured by time or an item. One resolver on both the hero and the enemy side (the design's warning: do not build the asymmetric version).
3. **Resistance/immunity** for bosses and a few families.
4. **Per-run floors** (random floors phase 2) once the ladder is stable.
