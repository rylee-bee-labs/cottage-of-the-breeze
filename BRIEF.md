# BRIEF — Cottage of the Breeze

> Stage 1 of the studio handbook: the pitch. One page. The parts marked
> ✍️ are Rylee's to write; nobody else fills them in.

## In two sentences
I want a fun, low bandwidth way to be able to be curious when I can't do
anything but still want to do *something*. Sometimes that's a 30 minute
session I can get lost in, sometimes it's me wanting a playthrough to
accomplish something.

## The one verb
Explore.

## Feel
Three words: curious, fun, low bandwidth
Looks like: the VEFR studio's storybook style (the World Tree art).

## Who it's for
Me, plus anyone who likes to have a thing to *do* when they can't do anything.

## Size
Play time: a 30 minute sitting that turns into four hours (what CoTW was
for me). Basically endless, like the dungeon in Stardew Valley, but with a
resolve at some point, so there can be a part 2.
Build lane: VEFR world pack + new engine rulesets (Library, then Delve)
Platform: phone + desktop browser, one shareable file

## What we're rebuilding
The systems of *Castle of the Winds* (1989): a town to rest and trade in,
floors going down, weight-and-bulk inventory, spells learned and found,
cursed and enchanted things. See `DESIGN.md`.

## Not in this game
Quick combat, or complex controls and things to remember.

## Done when
I lost an evening to it, for sure.

## Stage
Current: **1 · Concept** · Next gate: you can say the game in two sentences
and still want to make it tomorrow.
